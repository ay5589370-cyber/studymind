Privacy

Privacy Policy

NexoraAI is built to help students turn their own study material into clearer notes and explanations. Account information, uploaded content, and generated study activity are used to provide the learning workspace and improve reliability.

Information We Use

We may use basic account details, authentication status, study prompts, uploaded document text, generated responses, and saved history when those features are enabled.

Your Control

You can choose free mode without account history, sign in to save activity, or contact NexoraAI to ask about your data.
